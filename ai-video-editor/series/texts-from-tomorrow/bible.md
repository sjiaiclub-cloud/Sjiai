# Texts From Tomorrow — series bible

**Logline:** Maya, 16, starts getting texts from an unknown number that knows what's about to happen. The sender claims to be *her*, one day in the future.

**Tone:** mystery with a pinch of comedy. Short, punchy lines. Each part ends on a cliffhanger with a vote.

## Characters
- **Maya** (lead). She's sharp, sarcastic and trusts no one. Red hoodie, headphones, hair in a bun. Her voice is quick and dry.
- **Jay** (best friend). Upbeat, loses everything, would follow Maya anywhere. Curly hair, glasses, green jacket.
- **Future Maya** (the texter). We only ever see her messages. Her texts are read out in Maya's voice with an echo.

## Rules of the world
- The texts always arrive one minute to one day before the event they warn about.
- Every time Maya changes something, the next text gets stranger. She's breaking tomorrow.

## Arc (rough, voters steer it)
1. The first warning comes true. The lights go out and Jay disappears. ← Part 1
2. The search for Jay, or Maya texting back for answers.
3. Future Maya makes a request that doesn't make sense.
4. Maya realises the texts stop if she ignores one.
5. Showdown: what is tomorrow trying to prevent?
