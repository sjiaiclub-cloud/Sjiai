# Still Unsolved: 7-day content plan

One Short a day, posted at the same time every day. After 2 weeks, check **YouTube Studio → Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube"** and move the time to your best hour.

| Day | Case | Hook (first 3 seconds) | Why it works | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 001 · The Wow! Signal (1977) | "Earth picked up a signal from space. It lasted 72 seconds. Then it vanished forever." | Space + aliens angle, real science twist | ✅ rendered |
| Tue | 002 · The Mary Celeste (1872) | "A ship was found sailing perfectly… with nobody on board." | Ghost-ship visuals, everyone has a theory | ⬜ |
| Wed | 003 · D.B. Cooper (1971) | "A man hijacked a plane, took $200,000, and jumped out. He was never found." | Heist energy, case officially unsolved | ⬜ |
| Thu | 004 · The Voynich Manuscript | "There's a 600-year-old book that nobody on Earth can read." | Visual, no gore, endless debate | ⬜ |
| Fri | 005 · The Bloop (1997) | "The ocean made a sound louder than a blue whale. Scientists had no idea what it was." | Has a twist ending (later explained) → great comments | ⬜ |
| Sat | 006 · The Flannan Isles Lighthouse (1900) | "Three lighthouse keepers disappeared. Dinner was still on the table." | Spooky, atmospheric, short story | ⬜ |
| Sun | 007 · The Taos Hum | "Some people hear a hum nobody else can. Scientists still can't find it." | Relatable "can you hear it?" comments | ⬜ |

**Before making each episode:** fact-check every claim with a web search and list the sources in the episode file. These hooks are drafts, so check the facts before they become scripts.

**Every upload:** use the generated title, description and hashtags, set the generated cover as the thumbnail (YouTube app → Short → Edit → Thumbnail), and pin the A/B comment.
