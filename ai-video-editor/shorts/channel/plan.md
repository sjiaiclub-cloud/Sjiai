# Who Did It? 7-day plan

One case a day, same time every day. Each case has 3 suspects, 1 clue and the answer in the pinned comment.
After 2 weeks, check YouTube Studio → Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube" and move to your best hour.

| Day | Case | Setup | The clue (on screen) | Answer | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 001 · The Rainy Night | Gardener (greenhouse all night), niece (asleep), butler (polishing silver) | Gardener's boots are spotless after a night of rain | A, gardener | ✅ |
| Tue | 002 · The Cold Tea | Chef (out shopping for an hour), maid (brought hot tea 5 min ago), son (asleep) | The tea is ice cold | B, maid | ✅ |
| Wed | 003 · The Stopped Clock | Victim's clock smashed at 11:15. Driver (at the garage until 11), cook (baking since 10), guest (arrived at midnight) | Guest's train ticket is stamped 10:45 | C, guest: arrived before 11:15 | ✅ |
| Thu | 004 · The Blackout | Power cut at 9 PM. Neighbour (reading a book all evening), sister (watching TV), nephew (asleep) | Sister says she watched her show until 10 | B, sister: the TV had no power | ✅ |
| Fri | 005 · The Locked Room | Key found inside a locked study. Butler (only he has a spare), cousin (in the garden), doctor (on a call) | Spare key hook is empty | A, butler | ✅ |
| Sat | 006 · The Snowy Cabin | Snow until midnight. Guide (never left the lodge), skier (got back at six), friend (asleep) | Skier's footprints are on top of the fresh snow | B, skier: walked after midnight | ✅ |
| Sun | 007 · The Birthday Cake | Victim's cake has 5 slices missing, but only 4 guests ate. Baker, best friend, brother | Brother has frosting on his sleeve and says he "doesn't eat sweets" | C, brother | ✅ |

**Making each case:** write it as `episodes/NNN-slug.json` following the rules in `channel.json`. Before rendering, double-check the clue is fair, so a viewer could solve it from the video alone.
